STEP 01 / 04

APK Upload & Data Ingestion

Drop an APK file or paste a Play Store URL. We extract AndroidManifest.xml, classes.dex, and signing certificates — entirely without execution. Nothing runs.

  • Manifest parsing
  • Certificate chain verification
  • DEX bytecode extraction
STEP 02 / 04

AI Analysis Engine

Androguard parses every permission, API call, and hidden file. Our XGBoost model — trained on the CICMalDroid 2020 dataset — predicts the malware family.

  • Static analysis with Androguard
  • XGBoost — 470 features, 93% accuracy
  • Family attribution (Cerberus, Joker, BankBot)
STEP 03 / 04

Risk Scoring & Detection Breakdown

Hybrid detection: rule-based heuristics and ML predictions combine into a single 0–100 risk score. You see exactly which signals fired.

  • Signature match — known malware hashes
  • Heuristic — rule-based scoring
  • Behavior — ML-derived risk
STEP 04 / 04

Visual Report & Explanation

Get an explainable report — why the app is risky, what it can do, package name, and file hash. Not just a "safe" or "malicious" tag.

  • Detailed capability breakdown
  • Downloadable PDF export
  • Saved to your scan history
